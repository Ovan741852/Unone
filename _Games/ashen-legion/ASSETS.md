# Original art and provenance

All eleven `.mesh.json` models were authored for this project by `tools/build_art.py`. They use geometric triangles, tapered prisms and cylinders with baked face shading in `assets/palette.png`. No third-party character mesh was copied. Model sources are reproducible, and no Blender installation is required.

`assets/key-art.png` was generated with the built-in imagegen tool and copied into this project from the tool's generated-images folder. The source image is preserved. It is an original underworld legion inspired by the angular, bold ink and color language requested by the user; it does not depict the characters from Hades.

Final generation prompt:

> Create a polished widescreen 1536x1024 dark fantasy game key art, no text, no letters, no logos. Original underworld legion inspired by the bold inked cel-shaded illustrative visual language of Hades game: angular graphic shapes, thick dark outlines, rich crimson, burnished gold, pale bone, spectral turquoise light. Three original troop heroes grouped on RIGHT TWO THIRDS: foreground female sword legionary wearing a full angular black and gold Greek helmet with crimson crest, long crimson cape, holding broad glowing sword and round shield; behind her tall spear lancer with gold segmented armor and very long spear; on far right a black armored cavalry knight on a formidable black horse with gold plated armor and crimson saddle cloth. Dramatic triangular composition, full silhouettes readable, low polygon angular armor design suitable to model in 3D. Background crumbling Greek underworld temple, faint huge horned god silhouette and ember sparks, almost black deep green shadows. LEFT THIRD very dark quiet negative space suitable for game title overlay. Cinematic warm rim light and jade supernatural mist, premium game splash illustration, not photorealism. Output will be used as the menu background for an original roguelike arcade legion game.

UI weapon silhouettes are original inline SVG paths. Audio feedback is synthesized by Web Audio in response to shot/lottery observations. Unone and its bundled Three.js renderer retain their respective repository licenses.
