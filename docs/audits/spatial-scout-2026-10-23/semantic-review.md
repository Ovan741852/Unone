# Independent semantic review of the proposed spatial Scout audit

Reviewed on 2026-10-23 by a fresh reviewer with no implementation-author background. This review inspected `audit.proposed.json`, `evaluation.proposed.json`, the Scout report contract and rubric, the sealed blind reports and operation ledger, the three current recorder captures, the informed workaround probe as text, and the bounded source/design review. No audit command was executed, no engine was built or run, no network or installation was used, and no covered or proposed artifact was modified. The only new artifact is this review.

## Decision

The evidence supports `needs_work`, with `observation` as a critical blocker. The two publication incompatibilities are concrete and repeatable in the saved evidence. Success after changing the fixture does not repair those product contracts. The evidence also supports the narrower positive conclusion that one fresh AI author understood and used the repository's public spatial geometry contract, including moving-target sweep, category masks, frozen versions and next-tick ticket delivery.

No mandatory rating change is required. Keeping the proposed 70.75 is defensible with the wording corrections below. Performance 3 would also be a reasonable calibration under the general rubric because CPU timings and resources are observable; retaining 2 should specifically mean incomplete attribution of spatial work, rather than a penalty for untested GPU acceleration or an assertion that only command wall time exists. Neither calibration changes the gate.

## Required corrections before final publication

1. **Acknowledge the target CPU performance evidence.** The successful current capture contains `value.performance`, including script/physics/observe phase timings, frame statistics, counts and Luau memory. For example, `representative-first` records two frames, total 1.006 ms, phase totals 0.767/0.027/0.130 ms and 1,114,144 Luau heap bytes, with explicit debug-build limitations. These are target measurements, distinct from recorder or subprocess duration. The current performance answer's discussion of outer command wall times is technically true but incomplete enough to mislead. Explain that the remaining gap is attribution of spatial execution/transfer/application costs and a meaningful bounded profiling workload. Do not make GPU timing a prerequisite for a CPU-scoped score; the rubric explicitly does not require every tool to expose GPU metrics.
2. **Replace “freezes 33 selected subject files” with “fingerprints 33 selected subject files.”** The subject list records hashes, not immutable source copies. Only the blind evidence directory has the reviewed 26-file seal. A source fingerprint and a sealed retained artifact are different guarantees.
3. **Retain the actual rerun provenance explicitly.** The three `*-current` captures are the reruns used for the assessment after unrelated native-player source changed. The older captures must remain historical/stale rather than having hashes silently refreshed or being described as executions of the final selected subject. The proposed report already references the current captures; the final prose should state this distinction alongside the source-fingerprint limitation.

## Integrity and independence limits

I independently recomputed all 26 blind manifest entries' SHA-256 values and byte lengths; all matched. The manifest hash is `17f65c726903c1c4011b69c7f261c18675806463c186022083d6d9103f9ede73`, matching `SEAL.txt`. This confirms consistency of retained artifacts, not that their author was independent, the executable matches a particular source checkout, or their semantic conclusions are true. The visible blind ledger is consistent with public-only exploration; the aggregate author remains informed and should stay labelled `informed`.

`coveragePercent=100` and execution/public-execution coverage describe evidence attached to all ten scoped rubric questions. They are not coverage of every API shape, coordinate range, engine feature, host or device. The closed numeric `possibleRange` is the evaluator's consequence of assessing every question, not a confidence interval or a certification. The aggregate may use informed public probes to support public-access findings, but it must not relabel them as fresh blind behavior.

The raw current captures also retain a timed-out top-level `--help` invocation while the narrower help/discovery calls and completed geometry scenarios succeed. A blanket claim that every subprocess passed would therefore be unsupported. The proposed report does not need to treat that isolated timeout as a spatial-contract failure, because the fresh blind bootstrap succeeded and the geometry evidence is separately complete.
